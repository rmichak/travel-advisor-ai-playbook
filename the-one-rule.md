# The one rule

## Strip the client's identity before you paste.

An AI assistant needs the trip. It never needs the person. Replace names with "the client" or "the couple." Replace booking numbers with "[booking]." The answer is just as good.

Paste text. Do not upload files.

| Safe to paste | Never paste |
|---|---|
| Who is traveling, in general terms (a couple in their sixties, a family of five) | Name |
| Dates and length of trip | Passport number, date of birth |
| Budget | Home address, phone number |
| Likes and dislikes | Loyalty number |
| The question they asked | Card or payment details, booking numbers |

## Two privacy settings, thirty seconds each

Both tools can use what you type to improve their models unless you turn that off.

- **ChatGPT:** Settings, Data controls, turn off "Improve the model for everyone."
- **Claude:** Settings, Privacy, turn off the model improvement option.

## Before you paste an email you wrote

Copy the text into a plain document, then edit it there. Your voice is in what is left.

| Delete | Keep |
|---|---|
| Client names (replace with "the client") | How you open and how you close |
| Booking and confirmation numbers | The phrases you always use |
| Dates of birth | Everything else |
| Addresses and phone numbers | |
| Anything about payment | |
