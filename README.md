# The Travel Advisor's AI Playbook

Free prompt kits and setup sheet from The Travel Advisor's AI Playbook, a half-day AI training for travel advisors by Empowerment AI.

The session is a live, four hour working morning on Zoom. Advisors use a free ChatGPT or Claude account to build three "hires": a Researcher that turns a client question into a brief with a list of things to verify, a Writer that learns their voice from three of their own emails, and a Chaser that drafts the follow-ups nobody enjoys writing. Every prompt, the saving instructions for both tools, an honest table of what free accounts do and do not do, and a side by side cost table are in the files below. Nothing here needs a paid plan or a credit card.

**The one rule: strip the client's identity before you paste.** An AI assistant needs the trip, never the name, passport number, date of birth, home address, phone, loyalty number, card, or booking numbers.

Every person, booking, and price in these files is invented. Free-tier facts were checked against the OpenAI and Claude help centers on September 15, 2026, and free plans change.
