# ChatGPT and an agent: what is actually different

From The Travel Advisor's AI Playbook (Empowerment AI). Free to use and share.

Everything you built this morning runs on a free ChatGPT or Claude account: three Projects, nine prompts, one scheduled nudge. That is the do-it-yourself version, and it is real work saved every week. An agent is the next size up. Here is the honest difference.

| Free ChatGPT or Claude (what you built today) | An agent (what gets built for you) |
|---|---|
| Waits for you to open a tab | Comes to you: on a schedule, or when something happens |
| Knows this chat and your Project's instructions | Knows your business: your voice, your brand, your open quotes |
| Writes | Writes and does: checks a sheet, drafts into your mail, updates a record |
| You copy and paste | You approve, it sends, from your address |
| Lives in a browser | Lives where you already are: a text, an email |

The line that matters: **it drafts, you approve, nothing reaches a client without your eyes on it.**

## What the free version cannot do, stated plainly

- Read your inbox or your CRM.
- Notice on its own that a quote went quiet nine days ago.
- Send an email to a client.
- Run at an exact time (free scheduled tasks run in a morning, afternoon, or night window, once a day, three at most).

Everything else in the morning, it does.

## The one rule still applies

An agent gets the trip, never the client's identity. The same stripping habit, the same safe half. A well-built agent refuses to store a passport number or a card if one is pasted.
